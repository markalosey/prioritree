## PART 1 — THE Q-PROBLEM-SPACE SYNTHESIS

### 1.1 The core state transition

**IN** [STATED, Q-purpose + Q-setup-arch]: (a) any list of items; (b) any comparison operator; (c) the votes, gathered during the run — "votes are data in/out."

**The five-mechanism chain** [the core's five named parts, STATED; the verb sequence from the purpose ruling — "run the pairwise permutations → store the votes, then tally"]:

1. **ITEMS loaded** — the list enters as data; the item set is the round's universe [STATED named; DERIVED: the set precedes scheduling].
2. **PAIRS scheduled** — the pairwise permutations of the item set under the operator [STATED]. Whether "permutations" means the n(n−1)/2 unordered pairs or the n(n−1) ordered presentations is [OPEN → R7].
3. **VOTES appended** — a comparator answers a scheduled pair through the ONE vote interface, human AND LLM alike [STATED — the ruled cure for the core↔comparator coupling]. The vote store is the only accumulating state [DERIVED — the core is stateless and the tally pure, so no other state may hold the round].
4. **TALLY** — a pure function of the stored votes [STATED — the one ruled core invariant]. Consequences [DERIVED]: the tally reads only votes — never the operator, never the schedule — so identical votes ⇒ identical rank, always; the tally is operator-agnostic (the operator's semantics act at vote-generation in the comparators' judgment, never at tally time).
5. **RANK emitted** — "a list out that is in a superior order" / "a ranked whole" [STATED].

**OUT** [STATED]: the ordered list is the ruled output. Whether scores/counts/confidence ride with it is [OPEN → R12].

**Character** [DERIVED]: a run is two-phase — a GROWING phase (schedule → ask → append, looping while under-quorum pairs remain) and a PURE phase (tally → emit, zero mutation). The family's own data-shape precedent: "append-only JSONL; state is a FOLD over the file, never a rewrite" (friction_ledger.py:46). Layering [STATED]: the machine lives in src/prioritree; CLI wraps, FastAPI serves, web fronts (the human comparator's vote surface — Q-frontend-stack's ruled stack). The LLM comparator's wiring at the upper tiers is [OPEN] (arrives with the service tier, not the library).

**Prior-art grounding** [VALIDATED]: the chain is a generalized **Thurstone (1927) scale constructor** — judges cannot report absolute magnitudes but reliably report discriminal differences; votes in → latent order out — with the comparison operator as the parameter, which is where prior art thins. The pipeline is **Chatbot Arena's production-proven shape** (Zheng et al. 2023; Chiang et al. 2024 — 240K+ votes from ~90K users at the paper's Jan 2024 snapshot; ~1.7M historical votes platform today). The product's premise is the field's founding result, not a hypothesis.

### 1.2 The input entropy classes (the prior-art bounds folded in)

1. **Comparator error (human AND LLM)** [DERIVED] — nonzero on both channels. The VALIDATED LLM-judge numbers [VALIDATED, B1 — the corrected figures, setups never mixed]: in the non-tie setup, GPT-4-as-judge reaches **85%** agreement with human preference — *above* the like-for-like inter-human **81%** ("even higher than the agreement among humans," the paper's own words); in the with-ties setup, inter-human is **63–67%** and GPT-4 also scores **~66%**. Pairwise mode vs single-answer absolute grading is equal-to-slightly-better, not decisive (85 vs 85 non-tie; 66 vs 60/59 with-ties; 87 vs 85 Arena) — but the pairwise regime is where the judge matches humans, and absolute grading is where judges degrade: the product's chosen regime is the supported one. Human noise is the older datum: the same pair twice may get different verdicts (fatigue, context, order effects — Thurstone's ground) [VALIDATED]. Whether the core absorbs noise by redundancy or detects contamination is [OPEN → R8 quorum + R13 bar].
2. **Intransitive cycles (A>B, B>C, C>A)** [DERIVED, structural; VALIDATED A5] — **structural certainty, not a bug**: pairwise majorities can cycle even under perfectly honest votes (Condorcet 1785); the Condorcet winner may not exist; a total order can be unwarranted by the data. Cycle-resolution policy is [OPEN → R11].
3. **Ties / abstentions** — the outcome vocabulary is unruled [OPEN → R4].
4. **Duplicate votes** — the stream is append-shaped; idempotency is unruled [OPEN → R9].
5. **Incomplete pair coverage** [DERIVED, structural] — a round can halt with k of the scheduled pairs voted; the tally stays well-defined at any coverage [DERIVED from purity], but the emit policy is unruled [OPEN → R12].
6. **Item-set mutation mid-round** — unruled [OPEN → R1: the immutable run forecloses it].
7. **Ambiguous operators** [STATED-adjacent] — the operator is generalized input; since the tally never interprets it [DERIVED], operator ambiguity degrades the votes while the tally faithfully ranks the misjudgments. Operator validation is [OPEN → R3's declaration form].
8. **Mixed human+LLM streams** — one interface is ruled [STATED]; same weight is NOT [OPEN → R5]; comparator-class provenance is [OPEN → R5].
9. **Adversarial votes** — no stated trust boundary [OPEN → rides later]. Adjacent validated caveat: Arena rankings can be shifted by a few hundred rigged votes [VALIDATED, B2's adjacent note]. Suisen's honest boundary: the birth tally assumes a trusted-comparator regime, stated not hidden, with identity stamps making future weighting retro-fittable.
10. **Degenerate inputs** — empty list, single item (zero pairs), duplicate items [OPEN → R2's refusal set].

**The LLM comparator's STRUCTURED bias fields — not white noise** [VALIDATED, B3/B4]:

- **Order/position bias** — verdicts flip on presentation swap: "Vicuna-13B could beat ChatGPT on 66 over 80 tested queries" purely by altering order of appearance, with ChatGPT as evaluator (Wang et al. 2023). The named cure: **ask both orders, average** (Balanced Position Calibration).
- **Verbosity bias** — longer answers win more (Zheng et al. 2023); the field's cure: **length-controlled win rates** (Dubois et al. 2024 — Arena Spearman 0.94 → 0.98).
- Also on record: self-enhancement bias (mixed evidence), self-inconsistency (same pair, different verdict across samples).
- Consequence [VALIDATED-derived]: the vote schema must carry the metadata that makes bias measurable — judge identity and class at birth; presentation-order metadata arrives with the web tier's display surface. The birth fence proposes canonical (id-sorted) presentation and defers both-directions probing [PROPOSED, F3] — the stamps keep that widening additive. **This is the trade-off the plan carries** (named again in §3.5).

**The bounds on "superior"** [VALIDATED]:

- **Arrow's ceiling** (1951/1963, A4): for ≥3 alternatives, no aggregation satisfies unrestricted domain + Pareto + IIA + non-dictatorship simultaneously — "superior" can never mean "the uniquely rational aggregate"; every tally is a priced choice of which axiom to sacrifice.
- **Condorcet cycles** (A5): a total order can be unwarranted even by honest votes.
- **Kemeny is NP-hard** (Bartholdi-Tovey-Trick 1989, SCW 6:157-165 — pages corrected; Young 1988 "Condorcet's Theory of Voting," APSR 82(4):1231-1244 — phantom citation corrected): the MLE ranking is computationally infeasible at scale; at scale every honest tally is an approximation.
- **The defensible claim** [DERIVED from VALIDATED]: comparative and empirical — pairwise + tally beats absolute sizing at equal budget; never "the output order is correct." The honest output of any noisy tally is (estimate, uncertainty) — deferred with the probabilistic models (F7's DEFER).
- **The production datum** [VALIDATED A2, corrected figures]: ACJ's adaptive pairwise judgment reaches reliability 0.93–0.98 (headline alpha 0.96, the 1,000-script study) vs 0.75–0.80 for absolute marking — "superior order than sizing individual items" has production evidence among human judges. For LLM judges, the premise holds in the pairwise regime specifically [VALIDATED B1].
- **The scale arithmetic** [STATED — tool-verified at discovery, file-governed]: unordered pairs n(n−1)/2 — 45 at n=10, 4,950 at n=100, 499,500 at n=1,000; ordered both-presentations n(n−1) — 90 / 9,900 / 999,000. At 10 s per human judgment, n=1,000's both-order sweep is 9,990,000 s = 115.625 continuous days. Full coverage is the gold standard at small n; the adaptive theory (repetition cures noise at a logarithmic factor — Feige et al. 1994, "Computing with Noisy Information," SIAM J. Comput. 23(5):1001-1018; near-exact recovery under persistent errors at O(n log n) — Braverman & Mossel, "Noisy Sorting Without Resampling," SODA 2008:268-276; adaptive selection needs ~d log n comparisons where round-robin needs ~n(n−1)/2 — Jamieson & Nowak, NIPS 2011:2240-2248; dueling bandits as the scheduling theory — Yue et al., J. Comput. Syst. Sci. 78(5):1538-1556 — all citations VALIDATED with the corrections applied) is the earned-later lane behind the scheduler seam.

### 1.3 Hard constraints and irreversible failure modes

**Constraints** [STATED]: **tally purity** — the one ruled core invariant (same votes in → same rank out, no environment reads); the **pure-logic data plane** — stateless scoring/ranking, votes data in/out, no DB at birth (persistence a service-tier ruling); **zero runtime dependencies** (measured: pyproject dependencies = []); the **construction environment** — the kumi harness, patch the one mutation verb, zero-ignore strictness, the naming law, five-field docstrings, content-in-configuration; the git-tracked record home; no worktrees. The append-only record discipline is the family's stated mechanism ("append-only JSONL; state is a FOLD"; "append-only is a property of the op set, not of filesystem permissions") — its application to the product's vote store is [DERIVED-precedent → R9 makes it product law].

**The two named hazard classes** [STATED on this tree's own record — the family's paid tuition]:

- **FR-MEM-0143 (parallel-write races):** two ops fired in parallel into one store; both returned OK; one record was SILENTLY LOST — "any parallel route-op pair is unsafe." Mechanism [DERIVED]: unsynchronized read-modify-write; **success-return ≠ durable landing**; detection requires read-back, not the write path's word. Product relevance [DERIVED]: a vote interface serving humans AND LLMs is by design a multi-actor surface — concurrent appends are the expected case, not the edge. The family's own cure is stated: "locked appends."
- **FR-MEM-0001 (unverified payload data-gaps):** a fenced payload inherited from an upstream record carried a wrong root and nearly rode through ("a stray tree birthed… the emission split from the configured tree"); caught only because the executor validated bytes against ground truth. Mechanism [DERIVED]: data entering an act without a ground-truth verification step. Product relevance [DERIVED]: the vote stream is exactly such a payload surface; whether a vote is verified against its registered pair at write time is [OPEN → §3.4 proposes YES, at write time].

**What is irreversible** [DERIVED]: **the votes are the single irreducible state.** Purity means tally and rank are always recomputable; nothing derives votes from anything else — they are empirical judgments. Vote-ledger loss/corruption is the one loss that cannot be recomputed: it forces re-asking, and a re-asked judgment is a new fact, not the old one. Git-tracked store homes recover committed votes; the uncommitted window is the irreducible risk. **A bogus rank consumed downstream**: the asymmetry — a refused emission costs a re-run; a bogus emission costs every decision made on the false order. **The purity dividend**: identical votes ⇒ identical rank, so rank divergence between two copies is proof of store divergence — the tally doubles as a divergence detector. **The silent-loss mode**: a write can return OK and be gone; without an independent read-back census in the loop, loss is silent and permanent — this is why §3 spends its strictness on the write path.

## PART 3 — THE Q-SOLUTION-SPACE SYNTHESIS

### 3.1 The one-stochastic-act split

**The vote is the ONLY stochastic act; everything else is deterministic code.** Why judgment cannot be deterministic [DERIVED]: a deterministic function's output is derivable from its inputs; the vote's content — "A exceeds B under operator O" — is precisely the fact NOT derivable from anything the system holds (the items' stored bytes do not encode their relative order, and if they did there would be no product — you would sort by the bytes; Q-purpose's whole premise is that discernment is what comparators do). The judgment enters from outside — human semantics or model policy. Technically: an LLM's output is a sample from a policy distribution; practically: a human's judgment is noisy. Votes are noisy measurements of a latent order, treated as data — never as ground truth. Both comparator classes enter through the ONE write [STATED]; comparator_class is a data field riding the vote, never a second interface. **The schema gate is the stochastic/deterministic boundary.**

| Act | Class | Why | What it refuses |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) The VOTE (elicitation, human or LLM) | **STOCHASTIC — the only one** | Its content is the fact the system lacks | Nothing on content — the gate asks "well-formed?", never "correct?" |
| (b) Pair selection (scheduling) | Deterministic (vote state + explicit seed) | Scheduling is information-gain selection; an LLM picking pairs adds no information and makes the stream unauditable | <2 items; the exhausted state (the DONE signal, distinct from error) |
| (c) Vote ingestion | Deterministic gates | Schema validation, idempotency, dedup, ground-truth lookups — mechanical | Every malformed payload, with expected/found/next-action naming the store's truth |
| (d) Tally | Deterministic (ruled pure) | The ruled heart | Nothing on content — the emission gate (e) refuses on coverage |
| (e) Ranking emission | Deterministic, idempotent actuator | A projection of (d); same vote state → byte-identical output; zero write target | The safe halt: under-coverage — never a partial ranking dressed as whole |
| (f) Ground-truth lookups (the run manifest) | Deterministic | Pure reads over the frozen manifest | Unknown ids — the refusal names the legal set |

### 3.2 The five-op interface (each op's refusals and hidden seams)

1. **register_run(items, operator, quorum=k) → RUN-{####}** — the immutable (items, operator) ground. Refusals: <2 items; duplicate ids within the payload; schema violations — all expected/found/next-action. Side effects: exactly one — the run-manifest append, frozen thereafter. **Hidden seams/forecloses:** no mid-run item mutation op exists (item drift is foreclosed by construction, not policed — an op set that cannot express a hazard is narrower than one that can but refuses); the operator folds into the run (standalone items would make cross-operator vote pollution representable; the run makes it unrepresentable).
2. **next_pair(run_id, seed?) → (item_a, item_b, pair_vote_count)** — the scheduler read. Pure; default seed derived from run_id → replays byte-match. Refusals: unknown run; the exhausted state → the **DONE signal, named, distinct from error**. **Hidden seams/forecloses:** the scheduling policy hides here (increment 1 ships complete round-robin; adaptive selection is a later earned upgrade behind this seam); the pair returns no state, no scores — elicitation purity: the comparator must not see the current ranking (anchoring/order-effect contamination is the psychometric hazard; the bare pair is the only valid elicitation context); comparator-chosen pairs are foreclosed (adversarial pair-gaming).
3. **cast_vote(run_id, pair, winner, comparator_id, comparator_class, notes?) → VOTE-{####} + post-append ledger count** — the only stochastic-side entry point, and the only op that mutates the vote ledger. Gates: strict schema; winner ∈ the pair's declared verdict set; both item ids ground-truth-checked against the run manifest AT WRITE TIME (FR-MEM-0001's discipline); comparator_class ∈ {human, llm}; provenance stamp on every line. Idempotency: a byte-identical re-send is a LOUD idempotent ack naming the original handle (never silent — FR-MEM-0143's lesson); a changed verdict from the same (comparator, pair) is a new record (a revote is a new sample — changed minds are information); clerical corrections ride supersedes: VOTE-{####}. Refusals: unknown run/item ids; winner not in pair; malformed payload — naming the legal set. **Never refuses on content plausibility** (the system has no opinion about votes — noise is data). Ingestion is independent of the scheduler: a vote is valid on any registered pair (the scheduler suggests; it does not confer permission — keeping them decoupled preserves the Parnas seam and serves both comparator classes). Side effects: exactly one — the append. **Hidden seams/forecloses:** any notion of score (nothing to leak through); the return carries the post-append count — a built-in self-verification leg; vote edit/delete unrepresentable (append-only); vote-time ranking access foreclosed. Abstention needs no record: a comparator who cannot judge simply does not cast; the pair stays under-quorum.
4. **read_vote_state(run_id)** → manifest, per-pair counts, per-item comparison counts, coverage/connectivity verdict, the full vote log — the census read-back instrument. Pure. Refuses: unknown run ONLY. **Never refuses on coverage** — the audit surface must read back even a broken state, especially a broken state; that is its job (the op that catches FR-MEM-0143-class silent loss and FR-MEM-0001-class gaps; every gate's evidence leg rides it). Side effects: zero.
5. **tally_rank(run_id)** → ranked list (ties as shared ranks, stable id-order presentation), per-item comparison counts, coverage verdict, algorithm version stamp — the pure projection. Idempotent: same vote state → byte-identical emission. Write target: nothing — the actuator emits; the caller lands the output. **Refusal — the safe halt:** coverage gate unmet — (i) any zero-coverage item, (ii) the comparison graph disconnected, or (iii) per-pair quorum unmet → expected/found/next-action naming the isolated items/components and the exact pairs that close the gap. Never a partial ranking dressed as whole; never an empty list (a named refusal is data; silence is not). **Hidden seams/forecloses:** the tally algorithm and the supersession/dedup weighting policy (both live inside this module); under-coverage confident lies; nondeterministic tie handling — a tie is reported as a tie.

**Why five, not fewer (the merge-refusals):** ops 4+5 must not merge (the audit read must never refuse on coverage; the emission must always refuse on it — one op cannot do both honestly); ops 2+4 must not merge (the comparator surface must not see state; the auditor must — merging leaks the ranking to the judge); op 1 must not shed the operator (cross-operator pollution becomes representable). **Total mutation surface: two append-only stores (run manifests, vote ledger), zero edit/delete verbs, corrections as new entries referencing the old.**

### 3.3 The tally for increment 1 — win-rate/Copeland, with the alternatives priced

**RECOMMENDED (proposed): win-rate (Copeland-style). Score_i = (wins_i + 0.5·ties_i) / comparisons_i.** Order-independent batch (satisfies the purity ruling verbatim); closed-form, zero iteration, zero tuning; **unbiased under uneven schedules** (each item scored by its observed win fraction — the scheduler's shape buys no points); degrades gracefully on sparse data; honest about its own limits (a 1.0 score on one comparison is exposed as thin evidence by the per-item counts in the emission).

**Priced out:**

- **Points (Borda-style accumulation) — refused as default:** it conflates exposure with strength; the ruled architecture names pair scheduling as a core noun, so schedules will be uneven — an item compared more often accumulates more points regardless of quality; raw points are biased by construction under this product's own architecture.
- **Elo — refused by the ruling itself:** an online sequential update whose final rating depends on the arrival order, not just the multiset — demonstrated numerically in the validated record: the same two games in two orders yield finals 1499.81984 vs 1500.18016, a 0.36032-point difference on the identical multiset [VALIDATED B5]. "The tally is a pure function of the stored votes" reads on the vote set; order-independence IS the purity. Sequential update is also stateful scoring, contradicting the stateless core.
- **Bradley-Terry — deferred, earned later:** statistically the strongest (weights by opponent strength), but it carries iterative numerics with convergence edge cases (undefeated items → unbounded MLE; disconnected graphs → unidentified parameters; regularization priors = a second design decision riding the first), and its superiority materializes only on dense connected vote graphs — not the birth regime (sparse votes, small n, k≈1 per pair).

**The Parnas fold:** the vote record carries NO score field; ingestion computes nothing; the tally lives in one module behind op 5. The pure-function ruling permits any tally — so the vote interface must not leak the choice, and here it cannot: there is no field to leak through. Swapping win-rate → BT later touches exactly one module; zero vote records migrate; zero comparators notice. The emission carries an algorithm version stamp (auditability without coupling).

### 3.4 The hazard fold-ins (the repo's learned frictions as design requirements)

- **FR-MEM-0143 → the lock-file discipline:** (1) ONE mutating op per store (cast_vote for votes, register_run for manifests) — the write surface is a single choke point; adopt the lock-file discipline at the vote ledger: **exclusive append held across count → append → verify** (the record home's own .junro/.junro.json.lock / .packets.jsonl.lock are the on-disk precedent). (2) A concurrent write REFUSES LOUDLY (expected: free ledger; found: lock held; next: retry sequentially) — a refused write is data; a lost write is corruption. (3) Honest boundary at birth: a single-process contract documented in the module contract; the service tier later owns serialization (no DB [STATED]). (4) OK lines are not evidence; the store is: cast_vote returns handle + post-append count (self-verification built into the return); the deep read-back rides op 4. The orchestration law above the core: **sequential writes over one run's ledger, always.**
- **FR-MEM-0001 → ground-truth validation at write time:** validate against the store's own truth, never the payload's provenance: (1) register_run checks the payload against itself (duplicate ids) — even trusted-source payloads are untrusted bytes; (2) cast_vote checks every id claim against the run manifest, every verdict against the declared set — the refusal names the legal set and the store's truth, so the gap is mapped, not merely blocked; (3) **the write returns what landed, not what was sent** — send-vs-stored is diffable by the caller.
- **The append-only law — the concrete form:** no edit op, no delete op; corrections are new entries referencing the old. Two correction forms, both append-only: **changed mind = a new sample** (both count — information); **clerical error = supersedes** (the old stands in the log, excluded by the tally's weighting — and that weighting policy lives inside the tally module, swappable without record migration).

**The Ashby check (where input entropy could exceed variety, and what absorbs it):** noisy/contradictory votes → the schema's variety matches the judgment space (any well-formed vote, any count — refusing the second sample would discard the psychometric remedy; the tally's fractional scores do the averaging); cycles → not errors, a mathematical certainty — the fractional arithmetic dissolves them into win fractions, ties share ranks, the cycle is named in the coverage data, and the system NEVER "resolves" a cycle by judgment (that would inject stochasticity into the deterministic plane); adversarial voters → honest boundary (the birth tally is not adversarially robust — trusted-comparator regime assumed, stated not hidden; comparator identity stamped on every vote makes future weighting retro-fittable with zero record migration; pair-gaming foreclosed structurally — the comparator never chooses pairs); item drift → unrepresentability (the immutable run; a changed list is a new run); malformed transport input → strict schema + expected/found/next-action (the refusal set IS the variety); **the confident lie** (partial information that looks complete — the worst failure class by detectability: hidden) → the coverage gate, its only absorber; scale (n² pair space) → complete round-robin at birth, the hidden scheduler seam absorbs growth without changing shape; tie entropy → shared ranks + stable id-order presentation, deterministic for replay.

### 3.5 The single most important recommendation (and the trade-off the plan carries)

**cast_vote's write discipline** — the single mutating op, with schema validation, ground-truth lookups at write time, loud idempotent dedup, minted handles, provenance stamps, and the built-in read-back (handle + post-append count). Reasoning, by irreversibility: every other choice in this set — tally algorithm, scheduler policy, tie rules, quorum policy — is reversible by construction: hidden behind a module seam, revisable without touching a single record (Parnas bought that cheapness deliberately). **The stored judgments are not reversible: votes are the product's only irreplaceable asset** — re-eliciting a judgment is not recovery, it is a different sample — and a ledger that silently loses records (FR-MEM-0143) or silently accepts unverified payloads (FR-MEM-0001) poisons every tally ever run over it, undetectably from the output side. The write path is where the design spends its strictness; everything else revises behind a seam.

**The trade-off the plan must carry, named:** the birth fence buys feasibility and honesty at three priced deferrals — (i) **unordered-pairs-once** halves the judgment cost and matches the human comparator's natural single look, but defers the measured order-bias cure the validated literature names (both-orders averaging); the schema's stamps keep the later probe zero-rework; (ii) **win-rate** buys purity, closed-form determinism, and schedule-unbiasedness, but defers the statistical strength of Bradley-Terry — earned by a real noisy-vote dataset; (iii) **the coverage gate** refuses thin evidence rather than dressing it as a whole — the safe halt is honest but strict: one isolated item halts the entire emission, by design.
