## (2) THE READINESS PROBE — RECOMMENDED: the 404-as-ready probe, bounded

- The form: a retry loop, cap 10-15s, ~0.1s interval, each call GET /runs/RUN-0001/next with a short per-call timeout (~0.5s); transport refusal => sleep + retry; **404 => ready**; any other status => fail loudly. WHY 404 beats a raw connect poll: a connect proves only the listener accepts; the 404 proves the whole chain in one call — TCP accepted, HTTP parsed, route matched, the sync handler through the seam, the unknown-run refusal rendered, AND the store empty (the fresh home's live-set scan refusing RUN-0001). The probe doubles as the file's first refusal-arc data point. The boot's expected cost ~1-2s cold; the 15s cap ~10x expected — CI variance absorbed. The per-call timeout load-bearing: a connect landing mid-startup can stall in the accept backlog — short timeout + retry rides the gap.
