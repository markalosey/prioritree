- PATTERN 2 — THE CARD/TILE-CLICK (the one-click target's class; the survey platforms' single-question-per-screen + big-tile surfaces — the 2AFC preference tests' shape, the psychophysics heritage). - PATTERN 3 — THE RADIO-GROUP (the classic "choose one of N"): native semantics — the group announced as a group (fieldset/legend or aria-labelledby), the arrow keys ROVE AND CHECK within the group (native radio behavior), the checked state announced. TRUTH: the radio group is the semantically HONEST "choose A or B" (the question bound to the group at the announcement — the reader hears the group's name + "1 of 3"); BUT the radio's ONE-CLICK form is the cast-on-change, and THE ARROW KEYS THEMSELVES WOULD CAST — the keyboard user's browsing becomes the ballot (the misclick's keyboard cousin, a structural hazard the APG's radio pattern exists to manage); the radio+submit form (the convening's described shape) is the no-JS-safe form at two clicks.
